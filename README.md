# Innovatio

Science, space and tech, explained in short animated videos.

Innovatio is a YouTube channel of Shorts made with code-driven animation: journeys to the bottom of the ocean, through the centre of the Earth and up to the edge of space. Every video lists its sources.

## The YT Shorts Automation app

The channel uses a small private tool, YT Shorts Automation, to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel's owner and isn't offered to anyone else.
